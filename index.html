The 89 Meme

89, also known as the Eight Nine Meme, is a viral hand gesture and meme format that emerged as a parody and "antidote" to the popular 67 meme. Instead of the vertical 6-7 hand gesture, people wave their hands sideways or with palms facing down while repeating "89."

Origin: The 89 meme began circulating on TikTok in October 2025, with early examples showing people mimicking the cadence and style of 67 content but replacing it with "89." The gesture involves holding your hands horizontally with palms down, fingers pointing inward toward each other.

The Viral Moment: The meme exploded in late November 2025 when a video surfaced of the Grinch at Universal Studios Hollywood responding to kids saying "67" by squatting down, waving his hands sideways, and saying "89." This video became the defining moment of the meme, cementing 89's status as the official counter to 67.

Cultural Impact: Like its predecessor, 89 quickly spread across social media platforms including TikTok, Instagram, and X (Twitter). Users began creating their own 89 content, from simple hand gesture videos to elaborate skits. Major brands and influencers joined the trend, with some calling 89 the "67 antidote."

The Gesture: To properly perform the 89 gesture, extend both arms with hands horizontal, palms facing down, and fingers pointing toward each other. Move your hands up and down in alternating motions while saying "89." This creates the iconic look that has become synonymous with the meme.

Community: The 89 movement represents a playful counter-culture to 67, with supporters calling themselves part of the "89 crew." The meme has spawned countless variations, remixes, and creative interpretations, cementing its place in internet culture history.
